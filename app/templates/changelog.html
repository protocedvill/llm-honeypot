Changelog

June 3, 2026

Webhook retry policies for campus partners

  • AddedConfigurable webhook retry policies (fixed interval, exponential backoff).
  • ChangedDefault webhook timeout increased from 5s to 10s.
May 14, 2026

Saved searches & alerts

  • AddedGet notified the moment a new listing matches your saved search.
  • FixedDuplicate alerts sent when a listing was edited without changing price.
Apr 22, 2026

Payout history export

  • AddedCSV export for payout history, filterable by date range.
Apr 2, 2026

Listings performance

  • ChangedListing search results now paginate server-side; large campuses load ~4x faster.
  • FixedDate filter on the sold-items page ignoring campus timezone.
Mar 11, 2026

Campus partner roles

  • AddedCustom roles with per-resource permissions for campus bookstore accounts (previously fixed Admin/Staff/Viewer roles only).
Feb 20, 2026

API rate limit changes

  • ChangedDefault API rate limit raised from 60 to 120 requests/min on Plus and above.
  • FixedRetry-After header sometimes missing on 429 responses.
Jan 30, 2026

Instant payouts

  • AddedInstant payout option for verified sellers with 5+ completed sales.
Jan 8, 2026

Bug fixes

  • FixedDuplicate sale-confirmation emails sent when a payment retry succeeded on the same day as the original attempt.
  • FixedIncorrect fee calculation when a buyer used a promo code on a bundled listing.
