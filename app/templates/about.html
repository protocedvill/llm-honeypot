About Acme

Acme Corp builds billing infrastructure for subscription and usage-based businesses. Founded in 2019, we started with a simple observation: most invoicing software was built for a world of flat monthly plans, and it was buckling under the complexity of modern pricing — metered usage, seat-based tiers, hybrid contracts, and everything in between. Today, thousands of companies run their billing on Acme, from early-stage startups to public companies processing millions of invoices a year.

Our mission

We believe billing shouldn't be the reason a good pricing idea never ships. Our mission is to make it possible for any team to launch, iterate on, and scale a pricing model without rebuilding their invoicing stack every time finance changes their mind.
