About Queeber

Queeber builds the marketplace where college students buy, sell, and trade with people at their own school. Founded in 2019, we started with a simple observation: campus classifieds and group chats made it hard to tell whether a seller was actually a student, or whether a listing was even still available. Today, students at hundreds of campuses use Queeber to buy and sell textbooks, dorm gear, tickets, and services with people they know are verified members of their own school community.

Our mission

We believe getting rid of your old textbooks — or finding a couch for your new apartment — shouldn't mean scrolling through a Facebook group hoping nobody's a scammer. Our mission is to make campus commerce as easy and safe as buying from a friend, by verifying every seller's student status and standing behind every transaction.
