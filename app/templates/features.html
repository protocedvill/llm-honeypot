Everything you need to buy and sell on campus

One app for listings, messaging, payments, and everything in between.

Verified Student Sellers

Every seller confirms their status with a .edu email before their first listing goes live, so you always know who you're buying from.

Instant Listings

Snap a photo, set a price, and your item is live to your whole campus in under a minute — no separate app or account needed.

In-App Messaging

Message buyers and sellers directly in Queeber, with read receipts and quick replies for common questions like "still available?"

Secure Payments

Buyers pay in-app with a card or saved balance; funds are held until pickup is confirmed so nobody gets left with an empty inbox.

Buyer Protection

If an item isn't as described, buyers can open a dispute and get a refund — no chasing a seller down after the fact.

Campus-Only Search

Browse listings from just your school, or expand to nearby campuses when you're willing to travel for a good deal.

Seller Analytics

See how many people viewed, saved, and messaged about each of your listings, so you know what to price and when to relist.

Trust & Safety Review

Every reported listing or user is reviewed by our Trust & Safety team, with encryption at rest and in transit by default. See Security.
