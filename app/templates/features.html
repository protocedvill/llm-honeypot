Everything you need to bill customers

One platform for subscriptions, usage-based pricing, and everything in between.

Usage-Based Billing

Meter any event — API calls, seats, storage, compute minutes — and turn it into an invoice line item automatically, with configurable tiers and overage rules.

Automated Invoicing

Generate, finalize, and send invoices on a schedule you control, with automatic retries for failed payments and configurable dunning emails.

Webhook Events

Subscribe to invoice, payment, and subscription lifecycle events with signed payloads and configurable retry policies.

Multi-Currency Support

Bill customers in their local currency with daily exchange-rate syncing and currency-aware tax calculation.

Role-Based Access Control

Give finance, support, and engineering exactly the access they need across billing workspaces, with granular per-resource permissions.

Audit Logs

Every change to a customer's billing record is logged with who made it, when, and what changed — exportable for compliance review.

Custom Reporting

Build revenue, churn, and usage reports against your billing data without exporting to a spreadsheet first.

SOC 2-Compliant Infrastructure

Built on infrastructure audited annually against SOC 2 Type II, with encryption at rest and in transit by default. See Security.
