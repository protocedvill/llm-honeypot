Billing questions

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes — upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What counts as an "invoice" for plan limits?

Any invoice generated through the API, dashboard, or a recurring subscription schedule. Draft invoices that are never finalized don't count.

Do you offer annual billing?

Growth and Business plans can be billed annually for a 15% discount. Contact sales to switch.
