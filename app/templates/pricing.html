Pricing questions

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes — upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What counts toward my active listing limit?

Any listing that's currently live and unsold. Sold, expired, or removed listings don't count.

Do you offer discounts for student orgs?

Campus Partner plans can be billed per semester or per year for a discount. Contact sales to switch.
